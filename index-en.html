Projects

Launching a website for a building-materials store

An e-commerce platform for offline building-materials stores.

Product catalog
  • Gathered and analysed business requirements for moving the offline assortment into the online store.
  • Described the structure of a multi-level product catalog, categorisation rules and attributes for different groups of building products.
  • Defined requirements for search, filtering, display of product specifications, and synchronisation of the catalog, prices and stock levels with the accounting system.
  • Developed Use Cases, functional requirements and Acceptance Criteria for the catalog functionality.

Result: a content manager gained the ability to manage the catalog independently, without involving the development team.

Personal account
  • Gathered and analysed requirements for the user's personal-account functionality.
  • Described the business processes of registration, authorisation, viewing order history, reordering and managing delivery addresses.
  • Modelled business processes with Flow Diagrams and developed Use Cases for the key scenarios.
  • Aligned the logic of order-status display between the website and the internal order-processing system.

Result: users could track their order status independently, without contacting the support team.

Wishlist and product comparison
  • Analysed business requirements and described the logic of the wishlist and product comparison.
  • Developed functional requirements for saving selected products for both authorised and unauthorised users.
  • Described the logic of merging lists after authorisation and the rules for comparing products by technical specifications within a single category.
  • Prepared Use Cases, Acceptance Criteria and functional specifications for the implementation.

Result: fewer support requests asking "which one is better to choose".

Online product-return system

An online store for garden and gardening products.

Main tasks
  • Gathered and analysed business requirements for the product-return process.
  • Modelled the product-return process with a Flow Diagram.
  • Described return scenarios via a store branch or a delivery service (Nova Poshta).
  • Developed functional requirements for the return-request form (order data, return reason, return method, contact details, refund details).
  • Developed Use Cases, functional specifications and acceptance criteria for the implementation.
  • Defined business rules, form-validation rules and the request-processing logic.
  • Described the request-handling flow: from creation by the customer to verification, approval and completion of the return by a company employee.
  • Aligned requirements with the client, the designer and the development team.

Result: the product-return process was automated through the website, allowing customers to submit a return request remotely and choose the shipping method (via a store or Nova Poshta).
